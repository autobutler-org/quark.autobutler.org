# Invite family

**Coming soon.**

We are building a way for people in the same household to share one Quark —
their own logins, shared folders, and clear ownership — without turning Quark
into a public cloud account farm.

## What to expect later

- Invite someone who lives with you (or who you trust with home-network access)
- Separate accounts so each person has their own space
- Shared places for photos and files the household wants together

Exact steps and screenshots will land here when the feature is ready to use.
Until then, one owner account on the home network is the supported soft-launch
path. See [Set up Quark](/docs/start-here/getting-started).
