# Trash

Deleting from Files asks **Move to Trash?** and moves the item there. Open **Trash** from the app drawer.

## How long things stay

Thirty days. Quark checks every hour and deletes anything older than that for good. Each row shows how many days it has
left.

## Trash still uses space

Each drive keeps its own trash, on that drive. Something in Trash still takes up room until it is gone for good. If you
deleted things to make room and the drive is still full, open Trash and choose **Delete permanently** on what you don't
need, or **Empty trash**.

## Restoring and deleting

- **Restore** puts an item back where it was. If something new now sits there, move or rename it first. Restore never
  overwrites.
- **Delete permanently** removes it now, after you confirm.
- **Empty trash** clears everything shown, after you confirm. It sits at the top level of Trash only, and is grayed out
  when Trash is empty.

To act on several things at once, tap **Select** in the top bar, pick items or **Select all**, then restore or delete
them together. A message tells you how many were done.

You can open a trashed folder to look through it. Restoring one file from inside it brings back just that file, and
recreates the folders it was in if they are gone. The rest stays in Trash. Files in Trash don't open or preview.

## More than one drive

With more than one drive, device chips at the top of Trash pick which drives' trash you see. **Empty trash** only
empties the drives shown.

## What ends up in Trash

- Deletes from Files, one at a time or with **Delete selected**
- Photos you delete in [Photos](/docs/photos), including copies cleared out with **Duplicates**

## Who sees what

An admin sees everything in Trash. Anyone else sees what they deleted, plus things deleted from folders they can still
open. If a shared folder stops being shared with you, its deleted items leave your Trash too.

Trash updates live: delete something on your laptop and it shows up in Trash on your phone.
