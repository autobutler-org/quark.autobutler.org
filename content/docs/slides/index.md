# Slides (Beta)

Slides is a presentation editor that keeps your decks on your Quark. Each presentation is a `.qslide` file in Files,
next to everything else you keep there. Slides is on by default and marked **Beta**.

## Your presentations

Open **Slides** from the app drawer. It carries a **Beta** badge. The page lists every presentation you can reach,
newest first.

- **Search** matches presentation names straight away. After a pause it also finds words inside slides and speaker
  notes, listed under **Content matches**.
- **New presentation** asks for a name and opens the editor on a new deck with one title slide. An admin's new
  presentation goes at the top of the Quark; a member's goes in their own folder.
- Tap a presentation to open it. You can also open a `.qslide` file straight from Files.

## Editing

The editor saves as you go. A couple of seconds after a change, the save chip moves from **Save** to **Saving…** to
**Saved**. Tap **Save** to save at once. If a save fails, your changes stay on screen and the chip reads **Retry
save**.

What you can do:

- **Slides** — add, duplicate, delete and reorder them in the slide panel. Drag a thumbnail, or use its **⋮** menu.
- **On a slide** — text boxes, shapes, lines and arrows, pictures (from your device or from your Quark), tables, and
  charts. Select, move, resize and rotate them on the canvas.
- **Look** — five themes (Light, Dark, Warm, Cool, High contrast), slide layouts, backgrounds, and colors that follow
  the theme.
- **Transitions** — none, fade, push, wipe or zoom, per slide or for the whole deck.
- **Find and replace** across every slide.
- **Undo** and **Redo**.
- **Speaker notes** under the canvas, one set per slide.
- **Keyboard shortcuts** — press `?` or F1 for the list.

On a phone the same tools sit under the **Insert** and **Format** menus.

## Presenting

Tap **Present** in the top bar, or choose **Present from this slide** in a slide's **⋮** menu. The deck fills the
window.

- Step forward with the right arrow, Space or Enter, and back with the left arrow or Backspace. You can also tap the
  right or left side of the screen, or swipe.
- **Presenter view** shows the current slide, the next one, a timer, and your speaker notes. It needs a window at
  least 900 pixels wide, so phones don't get it.
- **Fullscreen**, or press F, in a browser or on a phone.
- Press Escape, or tap **End the presentation**, to go back to the editor on the slide you stopped at.

## PowerPoint in and out

**Import** — on the Slides page, tap **Import PowerPoint** (**Import** on a phone). Choose **From your Quark** or
**From this device**. Quark reads `.pptx`, `.pptm` and `.ppsx` files; it does not read the older `.ppt` format.

The new presentation is saved next to the PowerPoint file, with its pictures in a folder beside it. The PowerPoint file
itself is not changed. If anything in the file could not come across, Quark lists what was left out, and on which
slides, before the presentation opens.

In Files, a PowerPoint file's menu has **Open as presentation**, which does the same import.

**Export** — in the editor, tap **Export as PowerPoint (.pptx)**. Quark saves your latest changes first, then gives you
a `.pptx` file named after the presentation. Nothing in your presentation's folder changes.

So a Google Slides deck from [Google Takeout](/docs/files/google-takeout), which arrives as `.pptx`, can be opened in Slides.

## Sharing

Tap **Share** in the editor, or choose **Share** from a presentation's menu in the list. This is the same share sheet
Files uses. Pick a person or a group and a level: **Can view**, **Can edit** or **Owner**.

Someone with **Can view** can open the presentation, step through it, present it and export it. They can't change it.
The editor opens as usual, and the first time Quark refuses to save their change it switches to **View only**, with a
lock in the bar, and stops saving.

If two people edit the same presentation at once, the last save wins. There is no merging.

## For admins: turning Slides off

**Settings → Features → Slides** turns Slides off for everyone. The drawer row and the Slides pages go away, and so do
PowerPoint import and export and **Open as presentation**. Nothing is deleted: your `.qslide` files stay in Files, and
turning Slides back on brings everything back.

This switch is not a security measure. It hides Slides; it does not protect anything.
