# Photos

Photos gathers the pictures your account can open, from every drive, into one library. There is no special photo
folder. A picture you upload in Files, or unpack from a Google Takeout zip, shows up here too.

Photos is for images. Videos stay in [Files](/docs/files). The one exception is an iPhone Live Photo, whose short
motion clip plays inside the photo viewer.

You use Photos at home in a web browser, and in the iPhone and Android apps. Away from home, those phone apps reach
your library once an admin turns on remote access in **Settings → Network**. A web browser still works only on your
home WiFi. A big upload goes fastest at home.

Migrating from Google? See the [Google Takeout guide](/docs/files/google-takeout).

## How to use it

1. Open **Photos** from the app menu.
2. Scroll the grid. Months are the headings when you sort by date.
3. Tap **Search** and type part of a file name.
4. Tap a photo to open it. In the viewer you can move to the next photo in this view.
5. Tap **Upload** and pick the pictures you want. Quark does not copy your camera roll on its own.

## Browsing

- Photos sit under month headers such as **March 2025**. The header of the month in view stays pinned while you
  scroll, and a label naming the month floats at the edge of the grid. A photo with no date sits under
  **Unknown date**.
- The sort button in the top bar picks **Taken**, **Added**, or **Name**, each in both directions. **Taken** uses the
  capture date stored in the photo, and falls back to the date added when there is none. Under a name sort there are
  no month headers. Quark remembers your choice.
- The column slider, with **Larger photos** and **Smaller photos** at its ends, changes the grid size.
- **Search** finds every photo whose file name contains what you typed. Capitals don't matter. It does not stop after
  a few hundred matches.
- In the phone app, the **Showing** list picks **All**, **Quark** (photos on your Quark), **Mobile** (the phone's own
  library), or **Favorites**.

Pictures that belong to a slideshow are not in this library. Importing a PowerPoint puts them in a folder next to the
slideshow, with `_media` in the folder name. They stay in Files and in the slideshow. Move or rename the slideshow
away from that folder, and those pictures start showing up here.

## Adding photos

1. Open Quark and sign in.
2. Go to **Photos**.
3. Tap **Upload** and pick the photos you want. In a browser you can also drag photos, or a folder of them, onto the
   page.

Upload is something you do, one pick at a time. Quark does not back up your camera roll on its own.

- With more than one drive turned on, Quark asks which device the photos go to. They land in the top level of that
  drive.
- A dropped file that is not a photo is left out, and Quark tells you how many were skipped.
- If a photo has the same name as one already there, Quark keeps both.
- With one of your albums open, the photos you upload join that album too.

On an iPhone or iPad, uploading from **Files** can also offer **Photos** as a source, so you do not have to export
into Files first.

## Albums and favorites

Albums and favorites belong to your account. Nobody else sees them, admins included. You can't share an album. Share
the folder that holds the photos instead, from [Files](/docs/files).

- **New album** in the sidebar makes an album. An album's menu has **Rename**, **New sub-album**, and **Delete**.
  Deleting an album keeps the photos.
- An album name must be unique among the albums next to it, ignoring capitals, and can't contain a slash.
- Open an album and tap **Add photos** to pick photos for it. You can also **Select** photos and use **Add to album**.
  A photo's menu inside an album has **Remove from album**.
- The sort button beside **New album** orders your albums **A–Z**, **Z–A**, **Newest**, or **Oldest**. Favorites and
  the inbox stay on top.

**Favorites** is an album Quark makes for you. **Favorite** on a photo's menu puts it there. **Unfavorite** takes it
out. You can't rename Favorites or put another album inside it.

## A photo's menu and the viewer

A photo's menu has **Add to album**, **Favorite** (or **Unfavorite**), **Download**, **Share…**, **Make a copy**, and
**Delete**. **Delete** asks first, then moves the photo to the Trash.

Tap a photo to open it full size. In the viewer you can rotate it 90° clockwise (or press **R**), and the rotation is
saved on your Quark. The info panel shows the date and time, the location and camera when the photo has them, file
details, and the albums the photo is in.

**Download** on a phone saves a JPEG, PNG, GIF, WebP, HEIC, TIFF, BMP, or DNG into Apple Photos or the gallery. Quark
says **Downloaded** and the file's name. If you decline permission, it saves the file another way and tells you why.
The details are on [Files](/docs/files#downloading).

HEIC and raw camera files open in the viewer too. Your Quark converts them for display. The original stays as it was.

A picture you open from Files, rather than from this library, steps through that folder by name. See
[A photo opened from Files](/docs/files#a-photo-opened-from-files).

## Duplicates

On All photos, tap **Duplicates**. Quark groups copies as **Identical copies** (the same file) or **Similar photos**
(alike, but maybe edited or resized).

1. Tap a copy to switch it between **Keep** and **Delete**. A group always keeps at least one copy.
2. If the same picture is saved in more than one format, such as HEIC and JPG, **Keep: Any** lets you pick the format
   to keep.
3. Tap **Delete N photos** and confirm. The marked copies move to the Trash. Nothing is deleted for good.

A copy added outside Quark shows up once its thumbnail loads, or after your Quark restarts.

### While Quark is still reading photos

An admin sees a status panel at the top of Duplicates. Everyone else sees nothing there.

The panel is how you tell "no duplicates yet" from "Quark has not finished looking."

- **Still indexing, so results may be incomplete.** A pass is running. It also shows how many photos have been checked
  for duplicates, and how many dates have been read.
- **Indexing paused, so results may be incomplete.** An admin tapped **Pause indexing**. **Resume indexing** continues
  the pass.
- **Not every photo is indexed, so results may be incomplete.** The last pass did not cover the library.
- **All N photos indexed.** The pass finished. The panel names when it last finished.

If some files could not be read, the panel has a button such as **3 photos couldn't be read**. Open it for each file
and a short reason, such as "Can't be read as a photo" or "Too large to check."

## On a spare screen

A photo frame, a tablet, or any spare screen with a web browser can play one of your albums as a slideshow. The page
is small, so a modest screen can run it. Use a browser the screen already has. You do not change the screen's own
software.

This page is for your home WiFi. Remote access reaches Quark from the phone apps, not from a browser, so a frame in
another house will not open it.

1. On the spare screen, open your Quark in its browser, the same way you do at home.
2. At the end of the address, add `/frame`.
3. Sign in with your username and password. On a small screen, signing in can take about ten seconds. The page says
   so while it works.
4. Choose an album. The list is yours alone, and **Favorites** is one of the choices. Each row shows how many photos
   it holds.
5. The screen shows each photo whole, never cropped. It shuffles them, and moves on about every 15 seconds.

Tap the picture to open a menu: **Albums**, **Full screen**, **Sign out**, and **Close**. The menu closes itself
after a few seconds.

The next time you open the page, it starts the album you last picked, as long as you are still signed in. Sign-in
lasts the way it does in the rest of Quark. It ends after 30 days without use, and after 90 days no matter what. The
page remembers your username and the album. It does not save your password.

Sign in as the person whose albums should be on that screen. Anyone who can reach the screen sees that account's
albums. Tap **Sign out** when you are done if other people use the screen.

HEIC photos and raw camera files are shown as ordinary JPEG pictures. Videos in the album are skipped. If an album
has nothing it can show, the page says so.

If the screen can't reach your Quark, it keeps the picture it has, says so, and tries again. If the sign-in has
ended, it goes back to the sign-in form.

If the screen is set to reduce motion, photos change without a fade. The page uses Quark's dark look.

## Limits

- Your Quark won't decode an image over 64 megapixels, such as a stitched panorama. That photo has no thumbnail, and a
  HEIC that size can't be converted for viewing. The original still downloads as it is.
- Search matches file names. It does not look at what is in the picture.

## If something goes wrong

A tile with no picture is not always a missing photo. The badge says which:

- **No preview.** Quark has no thumbnail left to show. The file may have moved or been deleted.
- **Couldn't read this file.** The file looks damaged, or it is not the kind of file its name claims.
- **No preview on this device.** This phone or browser can't picture that format. A video in this state may still
  play, or play on another device.
- **Couldn't load preview.** Quark was busy or refused the thumbnail. The file may be fine. Try again later.

Other snags:

- **The library looks empty.** There are no images you can open. Upload one, or check Files.
- **A picture from a slideshow is missing.** It lives in the folder next to the slideshow. Photos leaves that folder
  out on purpose.
- **Duplicates look unfinished.** If you are an admin, read the panel at the top. A pass may still be running. Pause
  it with **Pause indexing** if the Quark is busy with something else, then **Resume indexing**.
- **The frame page asks me to sign in again.** The session ended, or this account has never signed in from the Quark
  app. Sign in once in the app, then try the frame page again.
- **The frame page is blank away from home.** It needs your home WiFi. The phone apps are what remote access is for.
  See [Remote Access](/docs/settings/remote-access).

## Tips

- A big first upload can take a while on WiFi. That's normal.
- Keep Quark powered on and the drive attached while large uploads finish.
- Irreplaceable photos still deserve a second copy. See the [Security guide](/docs/help/security-guide)'s 3-2-1 note.

## Nerd Notes

Checked against quark `main` at `c0f4eefc` (v0.56.2 plus one test-only commit).

**Library.** `GET /api/v0/photos` lists one page. `q` matches the file name, case-insensitive, and `total` is the
full match count. The old file-name search stopped at 500. This listing pages, so a search of 600 names returns all
of them (`TestPhotoSearch_PagesSixHundredMatches`). Sort keys are `taken`, `added`, and `name`.

**Slideshow media.** `ListPhotos` in `pkg/util/photoutil/list.go` drops any image whose directory is one
`pptxutil.MediaDirsOf` names for a `.qslide`. An import of `Talk.pptx` writes `Talk.qslide` and `Talk_media/`. A
renamed `Talk_(1).qslide` still claims both `Talk_(1)_media` and `Talk_media`. Moving the `.qslide` away leaves the
pictures in the library again.

**Index status.** Admins only. `GET /api/v0/photos/index-status`, `POST /api/v0/photos/index/pause`, and
`POST /api/v0/photos/index/resume`. A non-admin gets 403, and `PhotoIndexStatusSection` renders nothing unless
`AppSettings.isAdmin`. Failure reasons shown in the list come from `Errors.photoIndexFailure`: undecodable,
missing converter, too large, unreadable, or unknown.

**Thumbnails.** `ThumbnailIssue` in `quark_thumbnail_unavailable.dart` maps to the short labels **No preview on this
device**, **Couldn't read this file**, **No preview**, and **Couldn't load preview**.

**Decode cap.** `photoutil.MaxDecodePixels` is 64,000,000.

**Frame.** `internal/server/frame` serves `GET /frame` and `/frame/<asset>` as a small static page, ahead of the
Flutter app. It signs in with the same `POST /api/v0/auth/login` as the app and lists `GET /api/v0/albums`, which
creates that account's Favorites album on first use. Address options, read by `frame.js`:

| Option     | Values                              | Default   |
| ---------- | ----------------------------------- | --------- |
| `album`    | Numeric album id                    | Last pick |
| `interval` | Seconds, any integer greater than 0 | 15        |
| `order`    | `shuffle`, `newest`, or `oldest`    | `shuffle` |

`newest` asks `?sort=taken&order=desc`. `oldest` asks `?sort=taken&order=asc`. Shuffle randomizes that list in the
page. Photos matching `.jpg`, `.jpeg`, `.png`, `.gif`, `.webp`, `.bmp`, or `.avif` are shown as they are. `.heic`,
`.heif`, `.dng`, `.cr2`, `.cr3`, `.nef`, `.arw`, `.orf`, `.rw2`, and `.raf` are requested with `format=jpeg`.
Anything else, including video, is skipped.

The page stores `{ username, album }` in `localStorage` under `quarkFrame`. It does not store the password. The
session cookie is the app's: 30 days, sliding, capped at 90 days from creation (`sessionDuration`,
`SessionMaxLifetime` in `pkg/util/authutil/service.go`). The album list reloads about every 10 minutes. A failed
load retries about every 30 seconds. The on-screen menu hides after about 10 seconds.
`prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` drops the cross-fade. A legacy account that has not signed in from the app yet is
told to do that first.

**Downloads.** Same rules as Files: pictures and a short list of video extensions go to the platform photo library
on iOS and Android. See [Files](/docs/files#nerd-notes).
